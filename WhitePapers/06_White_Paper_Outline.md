# Proposed publication structure

1. **Abstract and limits of the result.** Brief, dated statement of the computation-only results through Y162. Explicitly distinguish recurrence, autonomous motion, an operational inertial proxy and untested physical equivalence.
2. **Research question and relational substrate.** Precisely state the local rewrite grammar, legal match conditions, causal conventions and vacuum measurements (MAIN §§1–3).
3. **Foundational obstructions and falsifications.** L, Q, R, S; unsuccessful engineering attempts; PRO's preregistered failure; applicability limits (MAIN §§4–8).
4. **The move to robust recurrent carriers.** Stage W/55744, producer-flow closure, full constituent turnover and natural-survival metrics.
5. **Operational measurement definitions.** Recurrence content, renewal clock and why the independent kinetic measurement requirement matters (COMP Y1–Y6).
6. **From motion to FBCL rest.** The X-series controls, Y7–Y48 kinematic development, signed speed, genuine stationary state and limited curvature result.
7. **Elementary and composite spectrum.** Bounded DS001 single-carrier census (Y49–Y53); CD001, compound structures, spectral census and G6 (Y54–Y82). Include explicit search bounds and blind-discovery protocol.
8. **Autonomous covariant kinematics.** Local role grammar, environmental reservoirs, independent native selector and the Y148 integrated engine (Y83–Y148).
9. **Inertial-action candidates and causal intervention.** Y149–Y159, including Y154–Y155 empirical correspondence and Y156–Y159 causal reinterpretation without conflating channels.
10. **Autonomous acceleration and present blocker.** Y160 intervention, Y161 period-2 state and limited reversal, Y162 transient collision; Y163 only after a completed report exists.
11. **Limitations, falsifiability and experimental priorities.** Include independent population Mi=αE0 test with held-out species, operational E0 definition, physical interpretation limits and gravity/coupling program.
12. **Methods, reproducibility and appendices.** Stage-by-stage record, seeds and source hashes, parametrized grammar, independent reruns, figures, claim-to-result traceability and negative-results ledger.

**Writing rule:** Do not turn an internal program gate (for example G6) into physical validation. Each substantive claim in the publication should link to a dated stage report and a particular table/log/figure, not merely to the prose summary.
