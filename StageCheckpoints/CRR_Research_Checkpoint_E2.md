# CRR Research Checkpoint Summary
## Gravity, Inertia, Recursive Phase, Entanglement, and Relational Rewriting
### Status through Entanglement Model E2

This is a continuity checkpoint for the speculative CRR research program developed in this conversation. It records the conceptual evolution, computational experiments, rejected approaches, surviving hypotheses, key equations, and the current research frontier so the project can be resumed even if conversational history is lost.

## 1. Original problem

The project began from a mechanistic question: if gravity is described as spacetime curvature, what is the underlying process that makes matter alter geometry, and why should the same matter exhibit inertia?

The accelerometer comparison was a key clue: inertial motion in deep space gives ~0 proper acceleration; free fall near Earth also gives ~0; standing on Earth gives ~1g. This suggests that gravity changes locally natural motion rather than exerting an ordinary local proper force on freely falling matter.

The first compact statement was:

> Gravity changes what inertial motion is. Inertia resists being moved away from it.

## 2. RCE — Relational Causal Equilibrium

The first working framework was Relational Causal Equilibrium (RCE): gravity and inertia are two manifestations of the reaction of an underlying relational state. Matter changes the equilibrium structure of that state; the resulting change in natural motion is gravity. Forcing matter to move contrary to that equilibrium structure produces a reactive response, experienced as inertia.

The central goal was already unification: do not separately postulate gravity and inertia if both can arise from the same substrate.

## 3. RPCD — Recursive Phase-Coherence Dynamics

After comparison with Fold Theory V and LaViolette's Subquantum Kinetics, RCE evolved into Recursive Phase-Coherence Dynamics (RPCD). Matter was viewed as a continuously recreated, self-maintaining coherent process rather than a permanent material object.

A provisional relation considered was

\[
m=\frac{\hbar\omega_0}{c^2},
\]

where \(\omega_0\) would be an intrinsic recurrence frequency.

The corresponding interpretations were:

\[
\text{inertia}=\text{phase-reconfiguration impedance of a self-maintaining pattern},
\]

\[
\text{gravity}=\text{modification of surrounding phase/coherence equilibrium},
\]

and weight was interpreted as coherence stress produced when support prevents gravitationally natural motion.

The equivalence-principle target was that one underlying structure should produce both an inertial observable

\[
m_i\leftarrow \frac{\partial^2 E}{\partial v^2}
\]

and an independently measured gravitational source strength \(m_g\).

The crucial future test became

\[
\frac{m_g}{m_i}=\text{constant}
\]

across different persistent structures without tuning.

## 4. External theories examined

### Fold Theory V

Useful ideas retained: coherence rather than substance as primary; recursive phase-locking; gluable neighborhoods creating proto-topology; coherence cones becoming light-cone-like structures; morphic tension representing departure from smooth recursive propagation; mass-energy associated with persistent coherence/gluing failure.

Main criticism: the conceptual text argues that metric/spacetime emerge, but the mathematical appendix later uses a conventional spacetime metric and curvature scalar in its action. The conceptual emergence was stronger than the actual derivation.

### LaViolette / Subquantum Kinetics

Useful mechanism: particles as self-maintaining nonlinear patterns continually recreated by a deeper process. The strongest retained insight was that acceleration requires restructuring a self-maintaining pattern, giving a possible microscopic interpretation of inertia.

Not adopted: literal etherons, Brownian/Fickian diffusion as fundamental, pre-existing Euclidean space and time, absolute preferred frame, and unsupported ether/superluminal claims.

### Cheol Woong Lee — The Complete Theory of Gravity

Useful conceptual nugget: hidden/internal phase + projection + motion/mass. However, major claims were rejected or left unsupported, including transverse Lorentz contraction, electromagnetic waves as special gravitational waves, mass-from-phase formulas that already contain mass, and the idea that Einstein's equation already contains electromagnetism and quantum mechanics.

The preferred interpretation became

\[
\text{higher-dimensional configuration/phase structure}
\rightarrow
\text{self-consistent modes}
\rightarrow
\text{effective 3+1D reality}.
\]

Literal fifth Cartesian space became less attractive than internal/configuration structure.

## 5. Pre-Wolfram substrate picture

Before Wolfram-style rewriting, the substrate was imagined as interacting cyclic processes

\[
z_i=A_i e^{i\theta_i},
\]

with recursion

\[
z_i^{(n+1)}=F(z_i^{(n)},z_j^{(n)},\ldots).
\]

Labels were not positions. Space would have to emerge relationally.

Persistent identity was represented by recurrence such as

\[
\Phi_{n+p}=e^{i\alpha}\Phi_n.
\]

The durable ontological statement that emerged was:

> The particle is not made out of the substrate. The recurrence is the particle.

## 6. Transition to Wolfram-style relational rewriting

Wolfram's framework supplied a more concrete pregeometric starting point without assuming primitive phase. The foundation became:

\[
\boxed{\text{pregeometric relational rewriting}}.
\]

A state is an abstract hypergraph

\[
H=(V,E)
\]

with local rewrite rule

\[
\rho:L\rightarrow R.
\]

No coordinates are initially assigned. Rewrite-event dependencies create causal ordering/proto-time; connectivity supplies candidate emergent space. The multiway graph represents alternative update histories.

The theory was renamed **Coherent Relational Rewriting (CRR)**.

Primitive statement:

> Reality is generated by local transformations of abstract relationships.

Central condition:

> Physically viable transformations exhibit recursive causal coherence.

## 7. CRR hierarchy and target observables

Working hierarchy:

\[
\text{relational rewriting}
\rightarrow
\text{causal coherence/confluence}
\rightarrow
\text{persistent recurrent/topological structures}
\rightarrow
\text{matter, phase/quantum behavior, spacetime geometry}.
\]

Matter is sought rather than inserted. A candidate particle is a persistent defect or recurrent structure \(D\) with an invariant

\[
I(D_n)=Q
\]

while its individual nodes/relations may change.

The original inertia/gravity observables remained:

\[
m_i=\left.\frac{\partial^2E(v)}{\partial v^2}\right|_{v=0},
\]

and an independently extracted gravitational/geometric source strength \(m_g\).

The central equivalence test remained

\[
\frac{m_g}{m_i}=\text{constant}
\]

for different persistent modes without tuning.

## 8. CRR Model 0A

Model 0A implemented coordinate-free hypergraphs, local rewrite rules, event-causal graphs, graph-volume dimension estimates \(N(r)\sim r^d\), homogeneity metrics, recurrent-motif detection, cycle-rank tracking, and finite-depth confluence tests.

Early binary and ternary rules frequently looked near-3D at small sizes. Longer scaling showed that most were false positives caused by densification, small-world shortcuts, low-dimensional branching, dimension drift, or eventual termination.

A critical methodological result was:

> \(d\approx3\) at one finite scale is nearly meaningless.

A credible emergent dimension must be scale-stable and checked using both local volume growth and global size/radius scaling.

## 9. Model 0A.2 — corrected Wolfram semantics

A major correction was made: the first engine had treated relations as unordered and forced distinct pattern variables to match distinct elements. Wolfram-style rewriting uses ordered relations and permits non-injective matches. Correcting this materially changed the generated geometries.

## 10. CRR-R14 — strongest background candidate from 0A.2

The strongest vacuum/background rule found was

\[
\boxed{
\{(x,y),(x,z)\}
\rightarrow
\{(x,y),(w,y),(y,w),(z,w)\}
}
\]

with fresh \(w\).

Observed properties included bounded mean degree about 2.95–2.97, extensibility through tens of thousands of events, and spatial dimension estimates clustering near three with finite-size/stochastic variation. Four 16k-event runs averaged roughly \(d_{space}\approx2.94\).

A specialized minimal-overlap causal test found 31 isomorphism-distinct competing branch outcomes, and 31/31 resolved by rewrite depth \(\le3\).

However, active perturbations generally spread through the network; perturbations that stayed localized did so only by being dynamically inert. R14 therefore became a promising vacuum/background rule but not yet a particle model.

## 11. Model 0A.3 — particle search

Binary triangular defects were tried first; they tended to disappear, replicate, or become trapped.

Higher-order relational structure produced a much more important result: an exact period-4 recurrent carrier.

## 12. Phase as cycle position

A P4 structure was found with a permutation \(Q\) satisfying

\[
Q^4=I,
\]

so

\[
\Phi_{n+4}=\Phi_n.
\]

This generated one of the strongest conceptual results of the project:

\[
\boxed{\text{phase}=\text{position within a closed recursive transformation cycle}}.
\]

For period \(p\),

\[
\theta_n=\frac{2\pi n}{p}.
\]

Phase therefore need not be fundamental. Recurrence can be fundamental, with phase emerging as a coordinate describing position in the recurrence.

Frequency becomes the rate of phase advance,

\[
\omega=\frac{d\theta}{d\tau}.
\]

This makes \(E=\hbar\omega\) suggestive, though CRR has not derived \(\hbar\) or that relation.

## 13. Particle identity as transformation orbit

The P4 experiments sharpened the particle ontology:

> Particle = localized relational configuration on a closed transformation orbit.

Supporting nodes may change while the recursive orbit persists.

At rest:

\[
F^p(\Phi_x)=\Phi_x.
\]

For a translating recurrence:

\[
F^p(\Phi_x)\approx \Phi_{x+\Delta x}.
\]

More generally,

\[
\boxed{F^p(\Phi)=T(\Delta)\Phi}.
\]

Interpretation:

\[
\Delta=0 \Rightarrow \text{internal recurrence/rest},
\]

\[
\Delta\neq0 \Rightarrow \text{recurrence plus translation/motion}.
\]

Acceleration would require changing \(\Delta\) while preserving recursive closure. This remains the most concrete proposed location for inertia:

> inertia = structural cost/impedance of changing the translational component of a persistent closed recurrence.

## 14. P4 success and mixed-rule failure

The P4 carrier could be persistent, localized, cyclic, mobile, and compact in the binary spatial projection. Its presence did not substantially destroy R14-like background geometry.

However, a serious problem appeared: P4 competing with itself was causally well behaved, but R14 competing with P4 generally failed mixed causal invariance. Broader simple carrier searches did not fix this.

This suggested a major design principle:

> Vacuum and particle should not be governed by unrelated fundamental rules.

A better architecture is one underlying dynamics with vacuum-like and particle-like recurrent solutions, analogous to one fluid equation supporting both quiet fluid and vortices.

## 15. Model 0A.4 — single-rule self-propagating fronts

A family of common-source rules was searched for rules that generate their own next rewrite front. A typical front behaves like

\[
(x,y,z)\rightarrow(w,x,y),
\]

so

\[
\Phi_n=(x_n,x_{n-1},x_{n-2}),
\]

\[
\Phi_{n+1}=(x_{n+1},x_n,x_{n-1}).
\]

This gave the interpretation

\[
\boxed{\text{motion}=\text{recurrence with relational displacement}}.
\]

A particularly interesting single rule was

\[
\boxed{
\{(x,y),(x,z)\}
\rightarrow
\{(w,x),(w,y),(x,w),(x,z)\}
}
\]

(sf99_009).

It had mean degree very close to 3, excellent minimal branch resolution (19/19 by depth 2), and one very large seed reaching about 512,000 nodes with global scaling estimates close to 3. Other seeds varied substantially, so it is interesting but not established as asymptotically 3D.

Perturbation lineage again spread broadly, so a selected nice rewrite front could not automatically be interpreted as a physical localized particle. Important lesson:

> A chosen rewrite sequence is not itself a particle. A particle must be encoded in the state or invariant structure.

## 16. Spatial projection versus internal configuration

This led to a richer relation model, schematically

\[
R(s,t,h_1,h_2),
\]

where \((s,t)\) determines ordinary spatial projection and \((h_1,h_2)\) contains additional configuration information.

Vacuum could be a symmetric/degenerate internal state while matter is a non-vacuum recurrent internal configuration.

This made it possible for spatial geometry and recursive coherence structure to be different projections of one underlying relational object.

## 17. Exact internal phase cycles and geometry tradeoff

Simple internal-routing searches produced exact period-2, period-3, and period-4 cycles, confirming algebraically that phase can be recurrence position.

However, the simplest exact-phase-capable rules tended to generate the wrong spatial geometry, often drifting toward effective dimension 4–5. Thus the simplest construction showed a tradeoff between good internal phase cycling and good near-3D spatial geometry.

This suggested that internal configuration must be more weakly tied to ordinary spatial endpoints.

## 18. Revised dimensional interpretation

The project originally treated \(d\approx3\) for the complete relational graph as a hard target. This was revised.

If the full state contains both spatial relationships and configuration/coherence relationships, there is no reason the full graph must have dimension exactly 3.

The more defensible condition is

\[
\boxed{d_{spatial}\rightarrow3}
\]

while the full relational dimension may be fractional, scale-dependent, or greater than 3.

A separate toy experiment confirmed that an exactly 3D spatial lattice can keep its spatial projection unchanged while sparse non-spatial coherence links raise the measured dimension of the complete graph.

However,

\[
d_{full}-3
\]

must not be interpreted directly as an “amount of entanglement”; shared constraints can actually reduce independent configuration degrees of freedom.

## 19. Entanglement E0 — phase locking versus shared recursive closure

A key distinction was tested.

### Classical phase locking

A pair with one definite but unknown correlated cycle position gives perfect classical correlation but remains a hidden-variable model. In the toy diagnostic it had zero negativity and no Bell violation.

### Shared recursive orbit

Representing the entire joint recurrence as one nonseparable state,

\[
|\Psi\rangle=\frac12\sum_{n=0}^{3}|n,n\rangle,
\]

produced Schmidt rank 4, 2 bits of entanglement entropy, negativity 1.5, and CHSH \(S=2\sqrt2\) when standard quantum machinery was used.

Thus the better CRR interpretation became:

\[
\boxed{\text{entanglement}=\text{shared nonfactorizable recursive closure}}.
\]

Mathematically,

\[
F^p(\Phi_{AB})=\Phi_{AB},
\]

while

\[
\Phi_{AB}\neq\Phi_A\otimes\Phi_B.
\]

Important limitation: Hilbert-space amplitudes, quantum observables, and the Born rule were imported for this diagnostic; E0 did not derive them.

## 20. Shared configuration space and dimensionality

Two independent period-\(p\) cycles have product configuration space approximately \(C_p\times C_p\), which approaches a 2D torus for large \(p\). A fixed relative-phase shared orbit is only a diagonal cycle \(C_p\), approaching dimension 1.

Thus stronger shared recurrence can reduce independent configuration dimension. Entanglement should therefore not be equated with an increased dimensional count.

Spatial separation can become large while configuration-space separation remains constrained: the two spatial projections may be far apart while still belonging to one joint recurrent configuration.

## 21. E1 — multiway recurrence and whole-orbit states

E1 tested whether causal/multiway equivalence itself forces the whole orbit to become physical.

Two independent updates

\[
A:(a,b)\rightarrow(a+1,b),
\]

\[
B:(a,b)\rightarrow(a,b+1)
\]

commute, so histories AB and BA reach the same endpoint. This demonstrates causal confluence.

But causal confluence does not identify different phase points such as \((0,0)\) and \((1,1)\). Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{causal invariance alone does not eliminate the hidden phase point}}.
\]

Introducing the shared recursion

\[
G(a,b)=(a+1,b+1)
\]

splits the 16 product states into four sectors labeled by relative phase

\[
\Delta=b-a\pmod4.
\]

If simultaneous global shift is treated as redundant, only relative phase remains.

## 22. Complex phase from recurrence spectrum

The real period-4 shift has eigenvalues

\[
1,-i,-1,+i
\]

(up to convention). Its symmetry modes are

\[
|\Delta,k\rangle=
\frac12\sum_n e^{2\pi i kn/4}|n,n+\Delta\rangle.
\]

This produced another major surviving insight:

\[
\boxed{\text{complex phase can arise as the spectral representation of real recursive motion}}.
\]

Complex numbers therefore need not be primitive substrate variables; they can emerge when a closed recurrence is represented in its eigenbasis.

## 23. E1 limitation — recurrence does not choose amplitudes

The same recurrence permits both a classical stationary mixture

\[
\rho_{mix}=\frac14\sum_n |n,n\rangle\langle n,n|
\]

and a coherent orbit state

\[
|\Psi\rangle=\frac12\sum_n |n,n\rangle.
\]

Both are recurrence-invariant. Therefore recurrence symmetry alone does not explain why nature uses amplitudes rather than ordinary probabilities.

Bare multiway path counting also fails: it produces positive multiplicities but no negative or complex weights.

So

\[
\text{multiway branching + confluence + path counting}
\]

is insufficient for quantum interference.

## 24. E2 — discrete rewrite holonomy

E2 introduced a real finite-state system with base configuration

\[
(x,y)\in\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2
\]

and internal recurrence coordinate

\[
q\in C_4.
\]

Two reversible rewrite operations were defined:

\[
X:(x,y,q)\rightarrow(x\oplus1,y,q+\alpha_y),
\]

\[
Y:(x,y,q)\rightarrow(x,y\oplus1,q+\beta_x).
\]

For

\[
\alpha=(0,2),\qquad \beta=(0,0),
\]

starting from \((0,0,0)\):

\[
X\text{ then }Y\rightarrow(1,1,0),
\]

\[
Y\text{ then }X\rightarrow(1,1,2).
\]

The base/spatial endpoint is identical, but the internal recurrence differs by

\[
\boxed{h=2\pmod4}.
\]

This residual transformation is a discrete holonomy.

## 25. Phase from holonomy

The \(C_4\) recurrence has characters

\[
\chi_k(q)=e^{2\pi i kq/4}.
\]

A holonomy \(h\) therefore appears in spectral sector \(k\) as \(\chi_k(h)\).

For \(h=2\): even \(k\) sees +1; odd \(k\) sees -1.

Thus different rewrite histories acquire a relative sign because they differ by an internal recursive transformation.

The chain is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{alternative histories}
\rightarrow
\text{residual internal transformation}
\rightarrow
\text{representation character}
\rightarrow
\text{phase}
}
\]

This is one of the strongest concrete mechanisms found so far.

## 26. Projective rewrite algebra

After transforming the internal recurrence into its spectral basis:

For even \(k\),

\[
T_XT_Y=T_YT_X.
\]

For odd \(k\),

\[
\boxed{T_XT_Y=-T_YT_X}.
\]

Thus a real deterministic finite-state rewrite system generates a projective representation in which alternative rewrite order differs by a phase.

## 27. Interference-like cancellation

If two alternative histories are coherently added with equal magnitude,

\[
\mathcal A\propto1+\chi_k(h).
\]

For \(h=2\) in odd sectors,

\[
1+(-1)=0.
\]

Exact destructive cancellation results.

However, E2 still assumes coherent linear addition. It derives the relative path phase, but not the amplitude superposition principle or Born probabilities.

## 28. Gauge invariance of holonomy

The internal coordinate was locally redefined by

\[
q'=q+\lambda(x,y)\pmod4.
\]

Individual rewrite-link shifts changed, but the closed-loop holonomy stayed fixed across all tested random local redefinitions. Thus the residual phase is not simply a coordinate-label artifact.

## 29. Generalized causal invariance

Before E2, CRR demanded exact branch reconvergence:

\[
A\rightarrow S,\qquad B\rightarrow S.
\]

E2 suggests a broader condition:

\[
\boxed{A\rightarrow S,\qquad B\rightarrow gS}
\]

where \(g\) belongs to an internal symmetry group \(H\).

The spatial/causal projections agree, but the histories differ by an internal automorphism. A state in representation \(R\) of \(H\) experiences \(R(g)\) as a phase or matrix action.

Exact confluence is the special case \(g=I\).

Provisional names:

- covariant causal invariance;
- confluence up to internal symmetry.

This is now a major candidate principle for CRR.

## 30. Speculative charge interpretation

Different representation sectors respond differently to the same holonomy. This suggests a possible future architecture:

\[
\boxed{\text{charge-like property}=\text{representation label of internal recursive symmetry}}.
\]

This is speculative only; no identification with electric charge has been established.

The best current working dictionary is:

**Fundamental reality:** local relational rewriting.

**Space:** large-scale geometry of a spatial/causal projection of that rewriting.

**Time:** causal ordering / recursive event structure.

**Particle:** persistent localized recursive mode / transformation orbit.

**Identity:** invariant recurrence, not fixed substrate elements.

**Phase:** position or spectral coordinate of recursive motion.

**Frequency:** rate of recursive phase advance.

**Motion:** recurrence with relational displacement.

**Inertia:** hypothesized cost/impedance of changing the translational component of a persistent closed recurrence.

**Gravity:** hypothesized change in natural causal/spatial propagation geometry caused by persistent relational structure.

**Entanglement:** shared nonfactorizable recursive configuration despite spatial separation of projections.

**Gauge phase:** representation of internal rewrite holonomy.

**Interference:** recombination of alternative histories carrying different holonomy phases.

## 33. Established constraints versus speculative results

Any final model must reproduce or respect established physics: Lorentz invariance, relativistic free fall and equivalence, quantum interference, Bell-inequality violations, no-signaling, spin/statistics, gauge structure, and Standard Model phenomenology.

Toy-model results actually demonstrated computationally include: finite-dimensional sparse relational networks; many false near-3D finite-size solutions; a strong R14 background candidate; exact finite recursive cycles; phase as cycle position; roots-of-unity phase from real recurrence spectra; shared relative-phase sectors; distinction between classical phase locking and nonseparable whole-orbit states; richer full relational dimension with unchanged 3D spatial projection; real rewrite holonomy; projective/anticommuting induced rewrite operators; and holonomy-dependent cancellation if amplitude addition is assumed.

Not yet derived: Hilbert-space linearity as a physical law, Born probabilities, measurement, Bell correlations directly from a CRR fundamental rule, spin-1/2, Standard Model gauge groups, physical masses, Newton's constant, Einstein's field equations, actual emergent inertia, actual gravitational attraction, the equivalence \(m_i=m_g\), or the observed particle spectrum.

## 34. Major rejected or superseded assumptions

The research progressively weakened or rejected: primitive material substance; primitive spatial coordinates; primitive complex phase; literal fifth Cartesian dimension; separate gravity and inertia mechanisms; separate fundamental vacuum and particle rules; exact \(d=3\) for the complete relational graph; exact microscopic confluence as the only acceptable branch relation; classical phase locking as entanglement; and positive path counting as quantum superposition.

## 35. Current central research problems

The main quantum problem is now precise:

> Why do physically possible recursive histories combine linearly as amplitudes rather than classical probabilities?

E2 supplies a mechanism for relative phase but not the amplitude principle or Born rule.

The main gravity/inertia problem remains:

> Can one persistent recursive mode independently produce both inertial resistance to changing propagation and causal/spatial geometric distortion, with a universal ratio?

The quantum and gravity/inertia branches now need to be reunited.

The strongest next step is to merge E2 holonomy back into the CRR geometry search. Search for rules satisfying:

A parallel quantum subprogram should investigate whether amplitude addition can emerge from representation theory, observer equivalence, conservation, or another invariance principle rather than being postulated.

## 37. Best compact statement of CRR at this checkpoint

> Reality is generated by local transformations of abstract relationships. Stable physical objects are persistent recursive modes of that rewriting. Spatial geometry is one large-scale projection of the relational structure; internal configuration structure need not be spatial. Phase is the spectral coordinate of recurrence. Motion is recurrence with relational displacement. Entanglement is shared nonfactorizable recursive closure. Alternative rewrite histories can differ by internal holonomy, whose representation appears as quantum-like phase. Gravity and inertia are hypothesized to be two responses of the same persistent relational structure: one changing natural causal geometry, the other resisting changes to its propagating recursive mode.

This remains a speculative research program, not an established physical theory. Its strongest achievement so far is the progressive replacement of vague words such as “phase”, “particle”, “entanglement”, and “coherence” with explicit relational and computational mechanisms that can be tested and rejected.
