CONTEXT

Stages D through R exhaustively characterized self-maintenance, translation,
and phase-bearing recurrence under the canonical Wolfram rule:

    (x,y),(x,z) -> (x,z),(x,w),(y,w),(z,w)

Two exact, architecture-level no-go theorems were proven along the way:

  - L (relation-floor theorem): under this rule, every clean internal
    producer transition satisfies rho_next <= rho_current -- the minimum
    relation count of a predictive class can never increase via internal
    dynamics. A destructive environmental event always drops it by at
    least 1, and it can never be recovered internally.

  - Q (causal-directionality no-go): every relation this rule ever creates
    points from older, already-existing material toward the single fresh
    node created in that event. No relation can ever point the other way.
    This makes any cross-coupling between two subsystems (e.g. a
    propagating core and an independent internal clock) strictly
    one-directional and therefore unrenewable once consumed -- proven to
    rule out ANY separate-event composition of propagation + phase.

R additionally found that self-generated maintenance (without L/Q's
theorems even entering into it) requires the two "frontier" targets of a
translating source to already share a direct edge with each other. Through
m=7, exactly one structure has this property -- the m=3, p=1 transitive
triangle -- and it has no internal phase (p=1). Every p>1 phase-bearing
recurrent structure found (428 phase states, m<=7) has independent,
non-adjacent frontier targets, and therefore cannot self-maintain by any
tested mechanism.

THE PROPOSED RULE CHANGE

Modify the rule to add one extra output relation: the fresh node w also
gets an edge pointing BACK to the retired source x.

    (x,y),(x,z) -> (x,z),(x,w),(y,w),(z,w),(w,x)

Rationale: this directly targets both theorems at their assumptions, not
just their conclusions.

  - Relation count per clean event goes from 4 relations -> 4 relations
    (net 0, the fact L's proof depends on) to 4 relations -> 5 relations
    (net +1). L's argument requires clean events to be relation-count-
    neutral; under this rule they are not, so the relation-floor
    monotonicity argument does not apply.

  - Every fresh node is now born with one outgoing edge (back to its
    retired parent), rather than being a permanent causal sink that can
    only ever receive edges. Q's proof depends on fresh nodes never having
    outgoing structure. That assumption is now false by construction.
