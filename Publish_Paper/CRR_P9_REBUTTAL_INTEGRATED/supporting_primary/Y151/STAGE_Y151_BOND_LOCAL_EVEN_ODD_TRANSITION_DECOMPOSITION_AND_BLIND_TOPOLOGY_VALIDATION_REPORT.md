1. A sign-even, bond-local, topology-sensitive forward transition response exists and composes exactly across multi-R5 N=5 chains.
2. A separate sign-odd R6 halo-dressing response exists and is not part of the cross-bond coefficient.
3. The independently frozen Candidate-B topology contrast is exactly proportional to the new transition-response topology contrast, while the Y99 causal-rest-content contrast has the opposite sign.

But it does **not** establish physical mass-energy equivalence. `M_cross` is signed and can be negative, DS001 has no bond-cross channel, and the total baseline scalings are different. A physically credible inertial observable would still need a positive/reciprocal action or susceptibility with a justified normalization.
