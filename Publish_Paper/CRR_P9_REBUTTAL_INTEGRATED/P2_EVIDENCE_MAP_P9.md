## Cross-claim dependencies

1. **Claims 3–8 depend on the FBCL/Y33 rule lineage.** They must not be presented as properties of the canonical Wolfram example rule, Rule 55744, or the original Rule-55584 carrier population. Rule 55584 and Rule 55744 are different Stage-W hits. Y48 shows the Y33/Y47 grammar shares 0/8 outputs with Rule 55584, while Y50 explicitly names the successful Y33/Y46/Y47 construction FBCL; Y47 and FBCL are therefore not separate laws.
2. **Claim 9 must use `M_curv`, not unqualified `M_i`.** Claim 12 establishes that independent inertial mass is operationally undefined.
3. **Claim 6 and Claim 13 are complementary, not contradictory.** Y129–Y131 concern the mature bond’s constitutive recurrence; D37 concerns an external environmental remnant encountered by another carrier.
4. **Claim 7 does not remove the causal-invariance limitation.** Intrinsic carrier update rules and relabel covariance are distinct from global event-order invariance.
5. **Claim 11 cannot be used to rescue Claim 12.** A topology-contrast correlation without a conserved transfer law or independent response is not rest energy or inertial mass.
6. **Claim 13 cannot reopen the energy ledger.** D37 never establishes conserved exchange, momentum transfer, or preparation independence.
